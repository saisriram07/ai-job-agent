How It Works

1

Period-wise Tracking

Attendance is automatically marked for each period throughout the day for all 20 students in the batch.

2

Daily Calculation

At the end of each day, the system calculates the minimum number of periods attended among all students.

3

Final Attendance

The minimum attendance count becomes the official attendance for the entire batch that day.

4

Silent Operation

The entire process runs silently without informing students or faculty about the tracking method.

📊 Example Use Case

Scenario: A batch of 20 students has 6 periods in a day.

Individual Attendance: Students attend 4, 5, 6, 3, 5, 6, 4, 5, 6, 5, 4, 6, 5, 4, 6, 5, 3, 4, 5, 6 periods respectively.

Minimum Periods: 3 periods (lowest among all students)

Final Batch Attendance: 3 out of 6 periods = 50% attendance for the day
